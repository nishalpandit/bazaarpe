How to Delete Your Bazaarpe Account

You may permanently erase your account either directly through the mobile application (instant) or by submitting the online web form below if you have uninstalled the app.

1

In-App Deletion (Instant)

  1. Open the Bazaarpe customer or vendor app.
  2. Tap on the Profile / Account tab.
  3. Select Privacy & Security settings.
  4. Tap Delete My Account and verify with OTP.
2
