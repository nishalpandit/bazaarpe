How recording works

  1. Order completed & vendor payment confirmed

    A payable to the user exists (backend ledger credit).

  2. Finance transfers money manually

    Outside this platform (bank portal / NEFT).

  3. Admin records the transfer here

    Payment date, method, reference and proof are required.

  4. Ledger debit shown as backend-calculated

    Duplicate recording is rejected by the backend.

Duplicate protection & audit

  • One settlement record per order per user; the backend returns a validation error on a second attempt.
  • Recording stores actor, timestamp, amount, method, reference and optional proof file.
  • Amount must equal the payable; over/under payment needs a separate adjustment entry.
  • No automatic bank transfer and no vendor payout flow is implemented.
