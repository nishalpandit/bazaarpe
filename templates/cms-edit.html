Is the 24-hour refund window guaranteed?

FAQ
Yes, all scrap pickups finalized through the Bazaarpe marketplace are covered under our 24-hour customer satisfaction guarantee. When a verified vendor completes your scrap collection, our calibrated digital weighing scale synchronizes the final net weight and rate calculation directly to your Bazaarpe dashboard. Key Protections Covered: - Accurate Weight Verification: You can cross-verify weights against the digital slip issued on-site. - Fair Material Grading: Metal purity and classification are locked per published daily market tariffs. - Dispute Window: If you notice any discrepancy, flag the order within 24 hours directly from your Order History screen or contact our 24/7 resolution desk. Our automated dispute desk reviews scale logs, vendor check-in photos, and disburses the corrected settlement adjustment into your bank account within 24 business hours.
